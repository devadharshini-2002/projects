QuickBite Express – Business Performance Analysis

Project Overview:

QuickBite Express is a Bengaluru-based food-tech startup founded in 2020. In June 2025, the company faced a food-safety incident and monsoon-related delivery disruptions.

This project uses Power BI to compare business performance before and during the crisis.

Analysis Period:

   Pre-Crisis: January–May 2025
   Crisis: June–September 2025

Project Objectives:

 Analyze changes in order volume and revenue.
 Identify affected cities and restaurants.
 Evaluate cancellation rates and delivery performance.
 Track customer ratings and ordering behavior.
 Identify changes in high-value customers.



Data Model:

The project uses fact and dimension tables:

Fact Tables:
 Orders
 Ratings
 Delivery Performance
 Order Items

Dimension Tables:
 Customer
 Restaurant
 Delivery Partner Menu Item
 Calendar

Dashboard Analysis:

1. Order Analysis:

   Compare monthly orders before and during the crisis.
   Identify cities and restaurants with the highest order decline.

2. Cancellation Analysis:

   Compare cancellation rates across both periods.
   Analyze cancellation trends by month and city.

3. Delivery & SLA Analysis:

   Compare average delivery time across both periods.
   Evaluate performance against the 36-minute SLA target.

4. Customer & Rating Analysis:

   Track monthly customer ratings.
   Identify customers who stopped ordering.
   Analyze changes in high-value customer behavior.
 
5. Revenue Analysis:

   Compare pre-crisis and crisis revenue.
   Analyze revenue changes across months and restaurants.

Key DAX Functions:

     CALCULATE
     FILTER
     DIVIDE
     RANKX
     ALL
     COUNTROWS
     AVERAGE
     COALESCE

Project Outcome:
      Created an interactive Power BI dashboard to analyze business performance and understand the impact of the crisis on orders, customers, delivery, and revenue.
